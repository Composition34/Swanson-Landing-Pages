Simple to Serve How to Use

Four unhurried steps from storeroom to sacred moment.

  • Open

    Lift the cups from the carton and place them on your serving trays.

    Tip: Chill ahead for warm days

  • Prepare

    Arrange trays at the front or hand sets to your serving team.

    Tip: One tray serves ~40

  • Use

    Peel the top foil for the wafer, then the second for the juice.

    Tip: Guide first timers gently

  • Store & Dispose
